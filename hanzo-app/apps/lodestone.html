    How it works

    Each document is tokenised, stop-words are dropped, and every remaining term is hashed into one of dimensions with a TF weighting. Vectors are L2-normalised, so cosine similarity is a dot product.

    Nothing is sent anywhere. This is a real, if small, retrieval index — the point is that the mechanics of RAG are simple enough to read.
